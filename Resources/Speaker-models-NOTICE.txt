Models are downloaded during setup. The native worker itself makes no network calls.
Audio is supplied as mono 16 kHz float32 PCM and processed on the user's computer.
Speaker numbers are meeting-local cluster labels; they do not identify a real person.
Cluster confidence is not a calibrated probability. Automatic speaker count is estimated,
with no hardcoded limit of three speakers. Short turns, noise, overlapping voices and
similar voices can produce merged or split labels; review and correction remain necessary.
