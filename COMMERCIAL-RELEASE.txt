PACKAGING LOCALVOICE FOR SALE
Research checked 6 November 2026. No store listing, payment service or paid
Apple account has been created. This release is still a development preview.

The practical first product is a native Apple silicon Mac app. Its global
shortcut and insertion into other Mac apps are native capabilities. A Chrome
extension would require additional development. A browser-only edition has
narrower reach; a companion extension can talk to a separately installed native
host. The current Swift app cannot simply be uploaded as a Chrome extension.
https://developer.chrome.com/docs/extensions/develop/concepts/native-messaging

For direct distribution, prepare a compiled app in a DMG, with Developer ID
signing, hardened runtime configuration and Apple's notarisation. New users
should install the app and download models in-app, without Terminal or compilers.
The current ad-hoc source installer does not meet that product experience.
Apple Developer Program membership is USD 99 per year; local pricing varies.
https://developer.apple.com/macos/distribution/
https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/

Suggested first release steps:
1. Verify this update on the developer's Mac, then test with 5-10 willing friends
   on supported Macs. Cover permissions, clean installation, cancellation,
   reopening, mode switching, real editors, battery use and memory pressure.
2. Replace source setup with a signed and notarised app installer, resumable
   in-app model installation, clear disk/RAM requirements and an uninstall path.
3. Provide a short privacy explanation, support contact, update process and
   purchase/refund terms appropriate to where it is sold.
4. Choose payment and licensing terms, then release a small paid version after
   the reliability checks pass. A one-off purchase is compatible with local
   operation: there is no per-minute cloud transcription bill. Distribution,
   support, payment processing and ongoing development can still cost money.

The downloaded AI models remain several GB even if the app installer is small.
No sales forecast or guarantee of performance is implied by technical feasibility.
